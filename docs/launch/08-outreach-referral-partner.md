# Outreach — referral partner

> **Draft. Not sent.** No email was sent to anyone.

**Why this one matters more than the cold contractor email.** A bookkeeper who serves thirty trades businesses hears about the missed-call problem thirty times a year, has already earned trust, and is not competing for the work. One good partner is worth a hundred cold emails, and the message is easier to send honestly — he is asking to be useful to their clients, not asking them to buy something.

**Every CASL rule in `07-outreach-contractor.md` applies here identically.** Same consent basis, same sender identification, same mailing address requirement, same unsubscribe, same log. Read that file first.

---

## Who to send it to

Ranked by how well the pitch actually fits:

1. **Bookkeepers and small accounting practices serving trades.** They see the invoice chasing, the missed quotes, and the admin hours directly on the books.
2. **Web designers and small marketing shops with contractor clients.** They get asked "can you make my site do X" constantly and cannot build X. Complementary, not competing.
3. **Trade associations and supplier reps** — fencing, landscaping, HVAC, plumbing. Slower, but one introduction reaches a room.
4. **Business coaches and BC small-business advisors** (including the WorkBC self-employment programs).
5. **Jobber / Housecall Pro implementation consultants.** They already sit inside the tools any build would connect to.

**Do not** approach the other Lower Mainland AI shops as partners. They are the competition, and there is nothing to trade.

## Notes

- **The honesty paragraph stays in.** A partner puts their own reputation behind a referral. If they find out afterwards that they sent a client to someone with zero delivered projects, that relationship is over and so is every referral behind it. Saying it first is the only version that survives contact.
- **Fill in the partner offer before sending.** ⟦Owner decision.⟧ A vague "let's figure something out" gives them nothing to say yes to. A percentage of the first invoice is the simplest and needs no tracking infrastructure at this volume.
- **Send a maximum of five of these a week** and follow each one properly. This is a relationship channel, not a volume channel — five real conversations beat fifty sends.
- **Have something to give first.** The five free calculators are a genuine gift to a bookkeeper's clients and cost nothing to hand over. Leading with "here's a tool your clients can use, no strings" opens more doors than leading with a referral fee.
