# 06 — Intent and Topic Clusters

**Researched:** 2026-07-31
**Machine-readable:** `05-keyword-universe.csv`

> **All volume, difficulty and CPC columns are blank by design.** No keyword tool, Search Console access or Keyword Planner access was available in this phase. Priority below is derived from *commercial intent × offer fit × competitor density × ranking feasibility for a zero-authority domain* — a reasoned model, explicitly not a measured one. Its limitations are stated in `15-content-gap-analysis.md`.

## Priority tiers

### TIER 1 — Build first (4 clusters)

**T1.1 — Missed-call capture (problem-aware)**
`missed calls contractors` · `losing jobs missed calls` · `missed call text back contractors` · `what does a missed call cost` · `contractor missed call calculator` · `stop missing customer calls`
Intent: problem-aware → solution-aware · Region: both · Page: `/missed-calls` (new pillar) + existing calculator
**Why Tier 1:** the problem the buyer actually names, less vendor-saturated than the product term, and an existing calculator already answers the money question with real math.
Cannibalization risk: **high** — must absorb `/use-cases/missed-call-automation`, `/how-to/automate-missed-calls`.

**T1.2 — Estimate & quote follow-up (best gap in the research)**
`estimate follow up contractors` · `how to follow up on a quote` · `quote follow up templates` · `automate estimate follow up` · `customers go quiet after quote` · `contractor quote close rate`
Intent: problem-aware → how-to · Region: both · Page: `/quote-follow-up` (new pillar) + existing generator
**Why Tier 1:** lowest competitor density found anywhere in this research; higher per-event value than a missed call (a quote is an already-qualified, site-visited lead); the generator tool already exists; and it is the workflow least served by platform-bundled AI.

**T1.3 — Ownership & lock-in (the moat)**
`do I own the automation` · `AI agency vendor lock in` · `one time build vs monthly subscription` · `what happens if I stop paying my automation agency` · `own your AI system` · `custom build vs SaaS small business`
Intent: commercial investigation · Region: both · Page: `/what-you-own` (new)
**Why Tier 1:** verified as a checklist-level buyer question; **structurally unanswerable by every subscription competitor**; directly converts the switcher segment, which is the segment that actually buys a build.

**T1.4 — Platform-gap ("I already have Jobber AI")**
`Jobber AI receptionist not working` · `Jobber AI receptionist review` · `Housecall Pro AI receptionist limitations` · `AI receptionist won't book into my calendar` · `connect Jobber to my phone system`
Intent: product-aware, post-purchase · Region: both · Page: `/beyond-platform-ai` (new)
**Why Tier 1:** effectively **zero competitor coverage** — no vendor writes "the AI you're renting isn't enough" — and it targets a buyer who has already spent money on the category and is dissatisfied. Highest-conversion audience available.

### TIER 2 — Build after core proof exists (4 clusters)

**T2.1 — Trade-specific office automation** — `HVAC office automation` · `plumber answering service` · `roofing company missed calls` · `fencing contractor quote follow up` · `landscaping office software`
→ **4 trade pages maximum**, each requiring a real client story before publishing. Absorbs 35 of the 39 current industry pages.

**T2.2 — Integrations** — `Jobber integration` · `Housecall Pro integration` · `QuickBooks automation contractors` · `connect Jobber and QuickBooks`
→ One integration hub + one page per named platform. Requires a working screenshot as proof.

**T2.3 — Trust, consent & recording** — `call recording consent Canada` · `PIPEDA call recording` · `CASL text message rules` · `is it legal to record business calls`
→ One Canada page + one US page. **Note:** Canadian competitors already cover this well; the differentiator must be *specificity to trades*, not the compliance claim itself.

**T2.4 — Cost of alternatives (honest comparison)** — `AI receptionist vs answering service cost` · `receptionist salary vs answering service` · `answering service cost per month` · `AI receptionist cost`
→ **One** page. The site currently has seven cost pages.

### TIER 3 — Long-term authority (3 clusters)

**T3.1 — Original benchmark data** — `contractor quote close rate benchmark` · `how fast do contractors respond to leads` · `trades response time study`
→ Requires running an original study. **Highest link-earning potential in the entire plan**, and the only route to the authority the domain currently lacks.

**T3.2 — Custom app / internal tools** — `custom AI app for small business` · `custom business software cost` · `internal tool development small business`
→ Low volume, high value, long cycle.

**T3.3 — Local (Metro Vancouver only)** — `AI automation Surrey` · `AI receptionist Vancouver` · `contractor automation Fraser Valley`
→ **Gated behind:** a live Google Business Profile, ≥1 named local client, ≥3 local citations. Until then these are doorway pages.

---

### WATCHLIST — interesting, unproven

- **Spanish-language US** (`servicio de contestación para contratistas`) — genuinely large US Hispanic contractor population, but no capability to deliver in Spanish. Do not build.
- **Quebec / French** — real market, but Voxify already holds it with Loi 25 positioning and native French. Do not build.
- **Voice search / conversational queries** — real but unmeasurable here.
- **AI-agent-to-agent discovery** — `llms.txt` already positions well; too early to invest further.

### REJECT — misaligned or misleading

| Cluster | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| **Creators vertical** (20 pages) | Different audience, vocabulary, price and proof requirement. Dilutes the entity — the #1 GEO factor — at the moment it most needs clarity. **Pause or spin out.** |
| **19 city pages** | No local proof, no GBP, no citations. Doorway-page risk under Google's own guidance. |
| **39 industry pages** | Claiming 39 verticals with zero delivered work in any is an unsupportable authority claim. |
| Generic "AI automation agency" terms | Saturated by better-resourced agencies; the site has four near-synonym pages competing internally. |
| "Best AI receptionist" listicles | Vendor-authored saturation; entering adds nothing. |
| Enterprise / SOC2 / compliance-heavy terms | Wrong buyer entirely. |
