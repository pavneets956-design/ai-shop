## Documented pricing traps

- **Dialzara** — $0.48/min overages
- **AnswerForce** — $2.00/min overages
- **Rosie** — $49 base plan does not include appointment booking
- **Setup fees $75–$1,500+**, "rarely disclosed upfront"
- **Ruby** — Capterra reviewer reported an expected $660 invoice reaching **$5,100+** with no advance warning
- **Smith.ai** — Trustpilot reports of AI auto-transferring to live agents **without client authorization**, inflating bills

**Read:** the ownership argument is strongest against the Canadian premium vendors and **weakest against Jobber's bundled $99 add-on** — which is precisely why `/beyond-platform-ai` matters more than a generic Canada page. Against the $14–$49 tier the payback argument does not work at all; against those, the argument must be capability and integration, not cost.
