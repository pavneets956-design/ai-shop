# 10 — Copy and Language Recommendation

**Basis:** 3,660 CA autocomplete suggestions · 22 live PAA sets · 334 Reddit posts · 2026-08-01
**Purpose:** this file unblocks the items frozen by §17 of the research brief — homepage H1, supporting copy, navigation labels, problem-selector labels, product names, CTAs.

---

## 1. Homepage H1

The frozen decision was between the brief's draft and "a stronger, evidence-based variation".

**Draft in the design brief:**
> "The office work slowing your contracting business down—handled."

**Assessment:** structurally fine, and "contracting business" is on-vocabulary. But "office work" is category language, not customer language, and "handled" is a claim rather than a specific.

**Recommended H1:**
> ## Quotes go quiet. Calls get missed. We install the system that catches them.

Why, from evidence:
- **"Quotes"** — 104 CA autocomplete variants vs 23 for "estimate" (4.5 : 1)
- **"go quiet"** — the searchable rendering of "Homeowners ghosting" (r/Contractor, 91 comments)
- **"Calls get missed"** — `missed call` is the largest cluster measured (347 variants)
- **"install"** — the differentiator vs the six Surrey competitors selling consulting
- Names two specific present-tense problems, which is exactly what *"Why is every construction 'AI' demo a Sci-Fi movie… when PDFs still ruin my day?"* (111 comments) demands

**Alternative, if a single-problem hero is preferred** (roofing beachhead):
> ## You already did the quote for free. We make sure it doesn't go quiet.

Draws on *"Tired of wasting time on free estimates"* (52 comments).

**Do not use:** "Done-for-you AI systems for contractors" as the H1. "Done for you business automation" returned zero contractor and zero Canadian results — it is internal vocabulary. It is acceptable as the *eyebrow*, where it clarifies the model without carrying search weight.

## 2. Supporting copy

**Recommended:**
> We set up practical AI and automation inside the tools your business already uses — for missed calls, quotes that go quiet, and repetitive follow-up. Your accounts, your data, your phone number. Export it any time.

Evidence: "the tools your business already uses" answers *"Does anyone use Jobber?"* and the "disappointed with Jobber" thread (112 comments) — the wedge is **configuration, not replacement**. The ownership sentence pre-empts the three verified objection queries (`who owns my customer data software`, `contractor software data export`, `can i keep my phone number answering service` — the last with `crtc.gc.ca` ranking).

## 3. Navigation labels

The design brief proposed: Tools · For Contractors · How It Works · Pricing · Proof · Get a Fit Check.

**Recommended, with evidence:**

| Proposed | Recommended | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tools | **Free Tools** | The "free" cluster is one of the largest measured. The word does the work. |
| For Contractors | **For Your Trade** | "Contractor" is right (283 vs 1), but a nav item on a contractor site saying "For Contractors" is redundant. Trade specificity is the open gap (`06`, Gap 4). |
| How It Works | **How It Works** | Keep. |
| Pricing | **Pricing** | Keep — and it is an attack-now target. |
| Proof | **Proof** | Keep the slot; there is nothing to put in it yet. See §7. |
| Get a Fit Check | **See what it's costing you** | "Fit check" is invented vocabulary with zero search presence. The recommended CTA is a leak-framing, matching how the tools work. |

**Route safety:** removing a link from the nav does not remove the route. `/demo`, `/shop`, `/creators`, `/ai-receptionist` must stay reachable from the footer so internal-link equity is preserved — `/creators` currently ranks best on the site.

## 4. Problem-selector labels

The design brief proposed three. All three are supportable, with one rewording:

| Proposed | Recommended | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| My quotes go quiet | **My quotes go quiet** | Keep verbatim. Matches "Homeowners ghosting" + the strongest PAA cluster. |
| I am missing leads or calls | **I'm missing calls while I'm on site** | "on site" is the contractor's actual situation; `missed call` is the largest cluster. |
| Office work is piling up | **I'm doing admin at 9pm** | "Office work" is category language. Specific and present-tense beats abstract. |

## 5. Product names

| Name | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Quote Rescue** | **Zero** occurrences across 3,660 CA suggestions, all PAA, and 334 Reddit titles. Keep as an internal/product name if you like it — but it must never be an H1, a nav label, or an SEO target. The searchable concept is "quote follow-up". |
| **Connected Office** | No search support either. Same rule. |
| **AI Receptionist** | **Keep.** 333 autocomplete variants. Pair with "answering service" (270) on first use — people price-shop the familiar category first. |
| **AI Business System** | Fine as an internal SKU name. Not a search target. |

**Rule:** product names are for sales conversations; page titles are for search. Do not let one become the other.

## 6. CTA language

| Position | Recommended | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero primary | **See what it's costing you** | Leak-framing; matches the calculators |
| Hero secondary | **Use the free tools** | "free" is a top cluster |
| Tool → paid | **Get this checked properly** | Bridge from self-serve to diagnosis |
| Final CTA | **Start with a free tool** / **Book a workflow check** / **Apply as a design partner** | Three honest actions; no fake urgency |

**Avoid:** "Get a Fit Check" (invented), "Book a demo" (nothing to demo yet), anything with "Transform", "Unlock", "Revolutionary", or a countdown.

## 7. Objection language — put it on the page

Four objections are verified live. Each needs a plain answer in the customer's words:

| Their words | Answer with |
|---|---|
| *"…or do they just piss off older customers?"* (50c) · PAA *"Why are customers averse to service chatbots?"* | **"Will this annoy my customers?"** — say what it does and does not do unprompted; give the owner an approval switch |
| PAA *"Can I keep my phone number if I switch provider in Canada?"* (crtc.gc.ca ranks) | **"You keep your number, in your name."** |
| PAA *"Who owns customer data?"*, *"Is our data really private?"* | **"Your accounts. Your data. Export any time."** |
| *"I'm really disappointed with Jobber"* (112c) · `how to cancel jobber` | **"Cancel any time — the system stays in your accounts."** |

---

## 8. Canada-specific terms

- **CAD explicitly.** Cost vocabulary is heavy and Canada skews cheap (`affordable answering service`, `cheap phone answering service`).
- **CRTC** is worth naming once on the phone-number answer — it ranks on that query.
- **Do not lead with "Canadian".** Jobber is Canadian (Edmonton); PAA asks *"Is Jobber a Canadian company?"*. Being Canadian is table stakes against the incumbent, not a differentiator. **"Surrey"** as a specific local claim is defensible; "Canadian" as a general one is not.
- Canadian spelling throughout.

---

## 9. Trade-specific variants

Roofing/exteriors is the beachhead and it is the open gap — only `askbenny.ca` is trade-specific across 34 SERPs.

Roofing page must answer *"Will AI replace roofing contractors?"* (live PAA) directly: **no — it handles the office work; you keep the trade.**

**Warning:** roofing SERPs drift to homeowner intent by PAA question 2 (*"How much does it cost to get a quote from a roofer?"*, *"How many quotes should you get for a new roof?"*). Roofing copy must be unambiguously addressed to the contractor or it will pull the wrong reader.

## 10. Words to avoid — evidence-backed

| Avoid | Evidence |
|---|---|
| "Home service business" | 1 variant vs 283 for "contractor" |
| "Done-for-you business automation" (as H1/nav) | Zero contractor and zero Canadian SERP results |
| "AI automation" as the lead category | 14 variants |
| "Estimate" as the primary noun | Outnumbered 4.5 : 1 by "quote" |
| "Fit check" | Invented; no search presence |
| Sci-fi / futuristic AI framing | 111-comment thread attacking exactly this |
| "Missed call text back" as an outbound opener | Contractors report being cold-called with it |
| "Trusted by hundreds", "Save thousands", "Guaranteed ROI" | Zero customers. Fabrication. |
| "Revolutionary", "Cutting-edge", "Seamless", "Transform your business" | Design brief §7 |
