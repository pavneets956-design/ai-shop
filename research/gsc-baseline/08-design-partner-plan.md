# 08 — Design Partner Plan

**Purpose:** acquire the first 2–3 real, nameable, measurable client outcomes — the binding constraint on everything else in this project.

**Why this is the priority:** the site earned **3 clicks in 46 days at average position 63.9**. Search is not an acquisition channel today and will not become one before there is proof. This plan is the actual growth mechanism; the website is downstream of it.

---

## 1. Target profile

**Geography:** Fraser Valley / Metro Vancouver, within ~45 minutes of Surrey — proximity is the point. On-site presence is what makes a reference real.

**Trades, in priority order:**
1. **Fencing / decks** — highest quote complexity, zero competitor targeting
2. **Landscaping / hardscaping** — best-evidenced in GSC (`/industries/landscaping-ai-automation`: 15 impressions, position 55.2; 3 landscaping queries)
3. **Concrete / paving** — high quote complexity, uncontested

**Firm profile:**
| Criterion | Target |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $300K–$2M |
| Staff | 2–15 |
| Existing software | Jobber or Housecall Pro (or spreadsheets) |
| Who answers the phone | The owner, or one overloaded admin |
| Quote volume | ≥15/month — enough for follow-up to matter |
| Owner disposition | Will answer a direct question; not looking for a silver bullet |

**Disqualifiers:** anyone who wants guaranteed lead volume; anyone who won't allow measurement; anyone who won't consider being named (that is the entire trade).

## 2. Baseline metrics — capture BEFORE building anything

Without a baseline there is no case study, only an anecdote. Capture across **14 days minimum**, ideally 30:

| Metric | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Inbound calls received | Phone records / carrier log | Denominator |
| Calls unanswered | Same | The core claim |
| Median time-to-callback | Manual log | Speed-to-lead |
| Quotes sent | Jobber / HCP export | Follow-up denominator |
| Quotes with ≥1 follow-up | Same | The actual gap |
| Quote → job conversion rate | Same | **The money metric** |
| Median days quote → decision | Same | Cycle length |
| Owner hours/week on admin | Self-reported, logged daily | Time saved |

**Rule: if it wasn't measured before, it cannot be claimed after.** No baseline, no case study.

## 3. Offer structure

**Discount is marketing spend, not charity — and it buys evidence, not goodwill.**

| Element | Terms |
|---|---|
| Standard price | Per `/pricing` (pending Decision 1 in `07`) |
| **Design-partner price** | **50% off the build fee**, first 3 clients only |
| What the discount buys | Named case study · recorded call · reference call · before/after metrics · logo/name usage |
| Care plan | **First 90 days free**, then standard |
| Payment | 50% to start, 50% on acceptance |
| Cap | **3 clients.** Stated explicitly — scarcity is real here, capacity is ~1 build/1–2 weeks |

**Never offered:** guaranteed revenue, guaranteed lead volume, guaranteed ROI, or any specific performance number. There is no data to support such a claim and making one would be the exact failure this whole research programme exists to prevent.

## 4. Scope boundaries — write these into the agreement

**In scope:** one primary workflow (call answering *or* quote follow-up — **not both**), integration with **one** existing system, up to 2 rounds of tuning in the first 30 days, handover documentation.

**Out of scope:** additional workflows, further integrations, ongoing content/script changes after handover, anything requiring a new third-party subscription the client hasn't approved, marketing or lead generation.

**Change control:** anything outside scope is quoted separately before work starts. No exceptions — scope creep on a discounted build is how a proof project becomes a loss.

## 5. Evidence and permission

Two separate consents, both in writing, both **before** work begins:

**A. Case-study permission**
- Business name, trade, city usable in marketing
- Before/after metrics publishable
- Owner's first name + last initial, or full name at their choice
- **Right of review before publication** — they see it first, and can request changes
- Revocable with 30 days' notice; published material removed on request

**B. Call recording permission**
- Explicitly acknowledges **PIPEDA all-parties consent** — the AI must announce recording to callers, and that announcement is non-negotiable
- Any published recording requires **separate consent from the caller**, obtained after the call
- Alternative if caller consent can't be obtained: publish a **re-enacted call using the real script**, labelled clearly as a re-enactment
- Customer names, addresses and phone numbers redacted from anything published

**Escalation ladder if a named case study is refused:**
1. Full name + business
2. First name + trade + city ("Dave, fencing contractor, Langley")
3. Trade + city only, metrics still real
4. Anonymised metrics with methodology published

Even level 4 beats what the site has now, which is nothing.

## 6. Measurement plan

**Day 0** — baseline locked, signed, archived. **Day 1–5** — build. **Day 5** — go live, tuning window opens.

**Day 30 review:**
- Same eight metrics as baseline, same method
- Calls answered vs. missed — before/after
- Quotes with follow-up — before/after
- Any failure modes, escalations, or complaints — **recorded honestly**
- 20-minute recorded interview

**Day 60 review:**
- Same metrics again — is it holding, or did it drift?
- **Quote → job conversion**, which needs 60 days to be meaningful
- Owner's unprompted description of the change (their words become the testimonial)
- Would they refer? To whom? *(This is the referral engine.)*

**Required artifacts:** dated screenshots of the baseline export and both review exports · a booking landing in Jobber/HCP · the AI's call log · system/error logs · the escalation path in action at least once · the recorded interview.

**Publish the failures too.** A case study that says "it mishandled 4 of 90 calls and here's what we changed" is worth more in a market where 42% of AI projects get abandoned than one claiming perfection.

## 7. Interview questions

**Baseline (before):** Walk me through the last call you missed that mattered. · What happens to a quote after you send it? · What have you already tried? What went wrong? · What would make you say this was worth it?

**Day 30:** What changed in the first week? · What did it get wrong? · What did you have to explain twice? · What do your customers say?

**Day 60:** Would you buy it again at full price? · What would you tell another contractor? · What's still broken? · Who else needs this?

*(Ask the negative questions genuinely. The honest answers are what make the case study credible, and the objections become site copy.)*

## 8. Case-study outline

1. **The business** — trade, size, city, how they got work
2. **The baseline** — real numbers, dated, with method stated
3. **What was breaking** — in the owner's words
4. **What was built** — plainly, including what it does *not* do
5. **What happened** — 30- and 60-day numbers side by side with baseline
6. **What went wrong** — failures, tuning, escalations
7. **The owner's verdict** — verbatim
8. **What it cost** — real price, no hedging
9. **Methodology note** — how measured, sample size, caveats. *This is the credibility multiplier.*

## 9. Publication conditions

Publish only when **all** are true:
- Written case-study consent on file
- Client has reviewed and approved the exact text
- Every number traceable to a dated artifact
- Failures included
- No guarantee, projection or extrapolation to other businesses
- Recording consent covers every voice published

**If any one fails, publish the anonymised version instead.** Never wait for perfect, and never publish beyond consent.

## 10. Success criteria

| Horizon | Target |
|---|---|
| 30 days | 3 design partners signed with baselines locked |
| 60 days | 1 published case study + 1 real recorded call live on site |
| 90 days | 3 case studies · 2 referrals · re-run the GSC analysis |
| 180 days | Proof sufficient to price at full rate and to make the Phase-1 consolidation decisions on real data |

**The 90-day mark is the checkpoint** for both the deferred URL decisions in `05` and the free-tools measurement — by then the tools will have had ~3 months of exposure instead of 13 days.
