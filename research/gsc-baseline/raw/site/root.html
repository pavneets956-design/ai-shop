02 / THE CHEAP-BOT QUESTION

Why not just buy the cheap AI receptionist?

Cheap AI receptionists are real — some answer calls, book jobs, even sync to your CRM. The software isn’t the hard part. Setting it up around your services, prices, calendar rules, and edge cases — and keeping it right as your business changes — is.

THE DIY TOOL
  • ✕The software may answer calls and book jobs.
  • ✕You configure the scripts, services, prices, calendar rules, and edge cases.
  • ✕You connect the apps and test the whole flow yourself.
  • ✕If it misbooks or gives bad info, you find out from the customer.
  • ✕Support is usually docs, chat, or a ticket queue.
THE HANDBUILT INSTALL
  • ▸I set it up around your services, prices, service area, calendar, and rules.
  • ▸I run real test calls before it ever touches a customer.
  • ▸I connect the workflow: call → request → quote → booking → invoice → follow-up.
  • ▸I tune it when your services, prices, or process change.
  • ▸You deal with the builder who installed it — not a ticket queue.
